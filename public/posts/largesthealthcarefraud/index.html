Largest Health Care Fraud Taken Down

Jun 30, 2025

The justice Department in US announced it’s results of 2025 National Health care Fraud takedown which caused criminal charges against around 300 defendants. The defendants include 96 Doctors, nurse practioner, pharmacists and other liscened medical practioners. This happened in 50 federal districts and exposed schemes involving over $14 Billion in intended loss. The takedown involved federal and state law enforcement across multiple agency in the country. This is the largest fraud ever done in US medical history with over $13 Billion in fraud money. It caused millions of dollars worth of property to be seized by the government. This operation prevented billions from being paid out through proactive data analysis by DOJ’s Health Care Fraud Unit.
