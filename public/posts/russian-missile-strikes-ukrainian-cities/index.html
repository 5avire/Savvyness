Russian Missile Strikes Ukrainian Cities

Nov 25, 2025

Russian missile and drone attacks have killed dozens of citizens including atleast 31 people in a ballistic missile strike on a residential apartment in Ternopil. The attack single-handedly created more than 100injured, and, many remained missing under rubble as rescue teams continue their search.

The strikes were one of the deadliest of the year and targeted densely populated areas. Ukrainian officials have deemed the attacks as war crimes. International reactions are renewing calls for stronger air defence support. The incident is highlighting the ongoing effect of the war on innocent civilians ; even those far from the front line.
