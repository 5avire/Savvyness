Historic Nobel Peace Prize Awarded to Maria Corina

Oct 10, 2025

On October 10, 2025, the Norwegian Nobel Committee notified the world of its intention by declaring María Corina Machado a Nobel Peace Prize winner. The Norwegian Nobel Committee stated that Machado won “for her tireless, non-violent struggle for the restoration of democracy in Venezuela.” A woman who has faced physical injury, bans from international travel, and political disqualification. To many, it means more than simply an individual’s honor; it can symbolize millions of Venezuelans who have struggled to be heard.

The response to the event was immediate, with polarized opinions. While the Maduro government described it as “colonialist interference” from the media, world leaders celebrated Machado, describing him as a symbol of civil bravery. However, the news also reveals the changing paradigm around which the world is becoming. The Machado news serves as a reminder to all that, despite all odds, even peaceful activism can bring change to the world.
