Capture of Nicolas Maduro

Jan 1, 2026

Codenamed Operation Absolute Resolve, United States forces launched coordinated attacks on Caracas and other strategic locations in Venezuela which resulted in 80 deaths. During the strikes, U.S. special operations captured president Nicolas Maduro and First Lady Cilia Flores. Maduro was immediately flown to NYC and he pleaded not guilty to federal charges there.

He pleaded not-guilty for drug trafficking and narcoterrorism. Following this abduction, Vice Prez Delcy Rodqiguez was sworn in to be the acting President while Madura is absent. This makes her the first female leader of Venezuela while the country entered a massive state of national emergency.
