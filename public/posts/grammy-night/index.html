Grammy Night

Feb 1, 2026

The 68th Grammy Awards saw three major achievements:

Kendrick became the most awarded hip-hop artist in history surpassing Jay-Z.

Bad Bunny secured first ever Spanish language album of the year for Debi Tirar Mas Fotos.

Steven Spielberg officially achieved EGOT status (Emmy, Grammy, Oscar, Tony) after winning best Music Film.
