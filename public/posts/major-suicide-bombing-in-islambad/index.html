Major Suicide Bombing in Islamabad

Nov 10, 2025

A suicide bombing outside a court in Islamabad killed 12 and injured more than 20 people. Pakistani Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi accused Afgan nationals to plan and carry out the attack. The tensions between the countries has escalated in the 2025 Afghanistan-Pakistan conflict. Pakistan is accusing the Taliban of sheltering militants. The attack underscored ongoing security challenges in Pakistan and further made situations more complicated about the cross-broder relations.
