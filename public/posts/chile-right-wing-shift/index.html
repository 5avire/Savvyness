Chile's Right Wing Shift

Dec 14, 2025

Jose Antonio Kast was elected as president in an decisive runoff against center-left candidate Jeannete Jara. Kast, leader of a Republican Party secured around 58% of the vote compared to Jara’s 42%. This victory marks a significant shift in perspective and ideological shift in Chile.

They have brought one of the most conservative government to power since it’s return to Democracy. Kast’s campaign was a platform of strict immigration rules and a better crackdown on crime. He is scheduledto replace President Boric and take the post from March 11.
