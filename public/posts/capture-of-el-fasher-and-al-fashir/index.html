Capture of El Fasher and Al Fashir

Nov 6, 2025

IPC officially confirmed famine in Darfur and South Kordofan on 3rd of November,2025. Sudan’s humanitarian crisis further escalated to devastating heights as international monitors announced that famine had officially struck parts of Sudan, including the regions of Darfur and South Kordofan. This announcement came at such a time when escalation of hostilities between Sudanese Armed Forces and RSF continued to prevent aid from being taken to those in need, including those without access to food, medicine, or shelter. At practically the same time, RSF forces staged yet another deadliest attack of the war thus far, targeting civilians in North Kordofan, including their shelter and funeral processions, resulting in dozens of deaths, although other sources indicate that the fatalities from these encounters may not be so low. This attack came on the heels of RSF forces capturing El Fasher, yet another major city in the region of Darfur, creating further mayhem in that region. These events underscored the direct role that the war is playing in bringing harm to Sudan’s civilians and underscored the need for unhindered humanitarian access into Sudan.
