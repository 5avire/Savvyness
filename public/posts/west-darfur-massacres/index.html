West Darfur Massacres

Dec 29, 2025

Rapid Support Force carried out ethnically motivated attacks in the West and the North Darfur during the Sudanese civil war. This killed over 200 civilians. According to the Sudan Doctors Union, the attacks targeted non-Arab populations.

This also included a clinic in the ZamZam displacement camp, and they were strategically targeted. These event represent some of the darkest chapter of conflicts. The RSF continues to consolidate control over the Darfur region through what others have opionated as genocide.
