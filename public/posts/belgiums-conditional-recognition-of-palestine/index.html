Belgium's Conditional Recognition of Palestine

Sep 2, 2025

In early September of 2025, Belgium gave a clear indication that conflict between Israel and Palestine would change significantly. In announcement of this new direction, the Belgian foreign minister stated that Belgium would recognize Palestine as a state at the United Nations, but only if conditions were met. These conditions would mean that there would be permission for hostages being held in Gaza to be set free, alongside Hamas being removed from power in Gaza.

At the same time, Belgium put pressure on Israel through the imposition of sanctions, such as boycott of Israeli settlement goods and limits on Israel’s participation in certain programmes. The ingredients were here for a debate about whether the conditions set by Belgium were realistic and whether the entire process might have an effect on the whole of the European Union, either through a harder stance or through a split opinion among some of the member states over the issue at hand.
