Brazil Jails Jair Bolsonaro as Coup Plot Sentence Starts

Nov 26, 2025

On November 25th, former Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro received a 27-year sentencing to a correctional facility following a conviction for orchestrating a plot to topple Brazil’s transition to a democracy after the 2022 election. The verdict marked the culmination of one of the most important cases of accountability in modern Brazilian history. The conviction of Bolsonaro and his associates proved that they had been planning to corrupt Brazilian institutions of democracy and had engaged in the planning of violent actions aimed at elected leaders. The action was perceived by both critics and supporters of Bolsonaro as a bold stand on defending democracy while others perceived it as political persecution.
