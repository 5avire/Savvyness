Gaza War - Israel strikes on water collection point

Jul 13, 2025

July 13,2025 , on a Sunday, an Israeli airstrike hit a water distribution point in Nuseirat refugee camp, central Gaza Strip. Palestinian health officials at AI-Awda hospitals reported the death of 10 people minimum. This included six childrens as well. Many wounded civilians queued for water due to severe shortage caused by this incident. The overall death in Gaza has reached well over 58,000 after this long period of conflict.

The Israeli force described this incident as a “technical error”. Be it technical or not, this caused massive harm to the civilians in Gaza. This incident also occured during a ceasefire. Although it was in ceasefire. the total death toll this day exceeds well over a dozen. Israel seems to not want a ceasefire if this incident was actually not a “technical error”. Wars can affect even the citizens and this should be well taken account into while such forces are being managed.
