On Sunday, the 21st of September, 2025, Australia, Canada, Portugal, and the United Kingdom officially recognized Palestine 
as a sovereign state. Canada became the first member of the G7 to make the move. 
This action can also be seen as a culmination of months of diplomatic pressure, 
which is greatly attributed to the Gaza crisis, famine declaration, and UN reports. 
This move greatly solidified the status of Palestine as a sovereign state while 
further distancing Israel from the rest of the world.
