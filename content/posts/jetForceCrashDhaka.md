Bangladesh Air Force FT-7BG! ( Chengdu 7-J variant ) training aircraft crashed shortly after takeoff from base.
It plunged into the Milestone School and College in Uttara,Dhaka. This incident lead to around 31 death and over 50 injured.
Some reports even cite upto 27 death among civilians and students.
This crash caused widespread destruction on the educational site drawing the attention of nation which lead to a nation wide mourning.
The investigation on the crash till now are not clear yet. However, it's believed to be mechanical orpilot error.
It ranks as one of the most deadly accident involving military planes in the recent history of Bangladesh.

As aviation technologies get more advanced, a crash can become even more fatal as it carries even more dangerous weapons and more fuel. More funds are being directed toward making safer air-vehicles. However, when it comes to machines made for war, there has to be a slight compromise on safety for the weapons which can be fatal. It'd be certainly more helpful for people to focus on safety rather than war and invest those war-funds into a better tomorrow.
