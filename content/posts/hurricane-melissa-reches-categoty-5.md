Hurricane Melissa strengthened into a Category 5 hurricane with winds of 185 mph and struck in southwest Jamaica on Sunday, October 7, 2025. The hurricane caused widespread destruction, power outages, and evacuations in the island nation, including reports of fatalities and flooding. A national emergency has been declared in Jamaica, and aid is being sent to the island nation as the hurricane threatens other Caribbean countries and the Gulf coast of the United States.

It's course is set to hit other countries as well and efforts to delay it and cause least harm are being made before it can infect the area. A Category 5 hurricane is not any joke and that it can cause serious destructions.
