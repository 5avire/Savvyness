In recent history, the most dangerous and most recent fatal crashing of a mine is the Rubaya Mine which collapsed on January.
The place was a critical hub for extraction of tantalum which is used in Devices.
Local Authority of Democratic Republic of the Congo confirmed that at least 200 people were killed.

The disaster has sparked the topic of safety of workers who work in mine and the lack of it.
The "blood-mineral" trade in conflict-prone areas of North Kivu is also experienced by this outrage internationally.
