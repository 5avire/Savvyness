Tensions concerning Greenland achieved a fever pitch as a result of allegations of a U.S. takeover plan by the Trump administration. 
The Danish government staged “Operation Arctic Endurance” by dispatching significant troop reinforcements to Greenland, a move emulated by the French government after a Defense Council meeting. 
Protests broke out in Copenhagen and Nuuk as the Danes marched through the streets bearing placards that read “Hands Off Greenland”. 

By January 21st, President Donald Trump, while attending the World Economic Forum, reached an accord with NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte, effectively ruling out the possibility of using military force and declaring that tariffs would not be issued to several countries in exchange for the new “Greenland framework”.
