On September-8,2025, anti-government protests in Kathmandu and major cities of Nepal turned deadly when police opened fire.
Normally, this was supposed to be a peace protest and there was supposed to be no gun shots.
However, Gen-Z anger over corruption and the protest not coming to a halt, the police opened fire, first with rubber bullets, then with real bullets. 
This enraged the public even more and the protest ultimately erupted with rage and did not back down until Prime Minister K.P. Sharma Oli resigned. 

The government also released the ban on social media that was placed upon the country.
Nakkhu Jail was also breached and several prisoner's were let free.
The country had entered a state of complete chaos. The chaos was later managed after the formation of the interim government and Sushila Karki was appointed as the interim prime minister. The event marked one of the most dramatic turnover of government within just only 3-4 days and flipped the whole government.
