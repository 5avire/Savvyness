title = 'Bondi Beach Terror Attack'

On Sunday, December 14, two shooters-identified as 50-year-old Sajid Akram and his 24-year-old son Naveed-opened fire during a Hanukkah celebration at Archer Park in Bondi Beach, Sydney. Motivated by extremist ideologies, 16 people including one perpetrator, died, and 42 others were injured. Victims ages ranged from 10 to 87 years old. Australian authorities found two improvised explosive devices at the scene and detonated them. In response, Prime Minister Anthony Albanese announced a massive national gun buyback program on December 19-the largest since the 1996 Port Arthur massacre-to tighten firearm regulations across the country.
