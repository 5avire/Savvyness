title = 'Massive Earthquake Hits Afghanistan'
+++

An earthquake of a magnitude around 6.0 struck near Jalalbad in Nangarhar province in eastern Afghanistan. Official reports confirm around 610 deaths and over 1300 injured.
This causes it to be one of the most deadly earthquakes in the country in recent history.

The victims were trapped by the debris of their homes and buildings in rural areas. The disaster added to the list of challenges facing Afghanistan, including food shortages and limited access to aid. Rescue work took several days, and aid groups from around the world offered to assist.
Despite so much efforts, victims are still in a state of suffering.
