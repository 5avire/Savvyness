title = "Tik-Tok's US Asset Sale"

In its efforts to avoid a ban within the US and its respective countries worldwide, TikTok and its parent company, ByteDance, have entered into a deal to sell off 80.1% of its US operations. A binding agreement has been reached with a group of investors comprising Oracle, Silver Lake, and MGX, who will take a 15% stake in a brand-new company called TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC.
It will also retain a minority shareholding of 19.9%, but be controlled by a majority-American board of directors.

Part of this deal will involve re-building the online recommendation system only using data from U.S.-based users and making sure that this data is stored and processed on computers within Oracle local systems. This will be aimed at addressing some of the concerns over the potential risks to the United States, China’s potential to access user information, as well as addressing concerns over issues of cybersecurity.
