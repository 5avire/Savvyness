The United Nations Security Council recently approved a US-drafted peace plan resolution for Gaza. 
The resolution backed the creation of an International Stabilization Force in case something breaks out again.
It's main task would be to oversee post-conflict governance and security.

This marked a rare consensus at the Security Council on Gaza after many months of unsuccessful attempts.
While people and critics are questioning the enforcement, troop contributions and other things, the vote reflected mounting an International pressure to move beyond just ordinary ceasefire and more toward a longer term success.
