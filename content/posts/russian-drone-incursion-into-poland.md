title = 'Russian Drone Incursion Into Poland'

Russian drone 250 km into Polish airspace during large-scale attack on Ukraine.
On Wednesday, September 10, 2025, 19 Russian drone strayed from their original attack path to Ukraine and entered Polish airspace. The Polish airforce along with NATO allies shot down four drones. This was the first direct engagement of Russian equipment to other country than Ukraine since war began.

The incident created quite a tension between NATO and Russia. 
This also raised fears of an even wider miscalculation and accident breakout of drones in other areas.
To counter this, a three month flight ban was kept over Polish airspsace.
