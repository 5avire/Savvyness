September-24,2025, UN Security Council voted to reinstate economic and military sanctions on Iran under the snapback mechanism of Resolution 2231 after several members of EU triggered it.
The countries to trigger it include :- France, Germany, United Kingdom, and some other.
This was done as Iran's nuclear escalation was non-cooperative with the IAEA, and failure to comply with JCPOA obligations.

This imposition is going to mark a major escalation in Iran's nuclear crisis and affect it's exports. Banking access, arms trade, and others are also affected.
It might also prompt strong condemnation from Tehran and it's other allies.
