Former President Yoon Suk Yeol was sentenced to five years by Seoul High Court.
The legal fallout from 2024 martial law declaration in South-Korea reached a milestone with sentencing the officials at top levels.
This was for obstructing justice and excluding cabin members.

In a more severe ruling days later, former PM Han Duck-soo was sentenced to 23 years in prison for his role as accomplice in providing legitimacy to the decree.
This will immensly help and has already started to reform and reshape South-Korea.
