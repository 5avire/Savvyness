Saudi Arabia, a major non-NATO ally, as declared by President Trump on November 18.
They have signed a deal that enables them to freely trade military weapons including F-35 Fighter jets.
The announcement is based on civil nuclear cooperation between Saudi and USA.

This move has signaled a significant strengthening of both nations.
It also signs toward a bilateral ties and a shift in the US Middle East strategy.
Supporters argue it is for enhancing security, critics are raising questions about human rights and arms proliferation. This grants Saudi and US preferential access to both countries resources and defence cooperation.
