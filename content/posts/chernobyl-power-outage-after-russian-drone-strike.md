The “emergency situation” at the Ukrainian-held nuclear plant near Kiev was announced by the Ukrainian Ministry of Energy on Wednesday, October 1, 2025. 
The declaration was a result of a Russian drone strike at the Ukrainian-held Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant, resulting in a three-hour power outage at the New Safe Confinement structure designed to contain radioactive materials from the 1986 nuclear disaster. 
The cause of the outage resulted from the destruction of a nearby energy facility in Slavutych. The generators were activated, and a state of panic ensued.
