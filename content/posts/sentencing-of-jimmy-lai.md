In landmark moment for Hong Kong's legal system, 78-year-old media tycoon Jimmy Lai was sentenced to 20 years in prison.
The cause for this was deduced as Conspiracy to collude with foreign forces under the national security law.
Six former Apple daily executives received sentences ranging from six to 10 years.

This has drawn widespread international condemnation from G7 nations and human rights groups.
