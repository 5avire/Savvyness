The one year formal withdrawal of the U.S. from WHO has officially been fulfilled.
Following this, the U.S. is no longer a part of the World Health Organization.
This makes United States the first country to ever leave the organization.
In a switch counter move, Californian Governor Gavin Newsom announced that California would independently join the WHO.

This is the first time that a U.S. state has been able to independently join an organization by bypassing the federal policy to maintain direct ties with the international health body.
