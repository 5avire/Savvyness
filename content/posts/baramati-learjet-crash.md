India was rocked by the death of Maharashtra Deputy Chief Minister Ajit Pawar and four others after a Learjet 45 aircraft crashed.
It hit the Baramati Airport in Pune.
The flight was reportedly attempting an emergency landing due to technical failure when it overshot the runway.

The death of a fellow high-profile political figure has led to a state of mourning in Maharashtra and an immediate federal investigation into the maintenance records of private charter fleets in India.
