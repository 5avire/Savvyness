title = 'Crans Montana Bar Fire'

A tragic disaster struck the Swiss ski resort of Crans-Montana on New Year’s celebrations when a fire broke out at a bar called Le Constellation. The fire, which is believed to have resulted from soundproofing foam catching fire from pyrotechnics used indoors, claimed 41 lives and injured 116 others, most of whom were teenagers and young adults. The magnitude of the fire prompted the declaration of a national day of mourning across Switzerland and called for a criminal investigation of the owners of the bar and local safety authorities who failed to carry out required fire safety inspections from 2019.
