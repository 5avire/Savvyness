Medecins Sans Frontieres withdrew staffs from a hospital located in central Darfur after repeated attacks on November 25.
The attacks also included the killing of a stretcher bearer.
This decision has come through after famine was officially declared in parts of Sudan.

This is highlighting the collapse of humanitarian access amid fighting between the Sudanese Armed Force and the Rapid Support Forces.
Aid agencies  have warned that civilians were being trapped without foods and protection.
MSF also stated that it would not return safety for the people is guaranteed.
