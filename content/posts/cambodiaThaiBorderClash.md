Armed clashes erupted near Prasat Ta Muen Thom temple along Cambodia-Thailand border.
This incident on Thursday, July-24,2025 caused thousands of displacement among civilians.
According to reports, at least 14 people were killed and 46 injured on the Thai side with additional casualties on both sides.
The cause for this incident was long standing tention between the two nations about border territory.
Thailand later on declared martial law in border districts, highlighting regional tensions and the need for diploatic intervention to prevent wide escalation of the incident.

The two nations exchanged fire, artillery, and rocket attacks with Thai even launching airstrikes into Cambodian territory. Events such as these should be dealt with through mutual understanding rather than going into cross fire. Going to such lengths just cause tragity among the parties involved.
