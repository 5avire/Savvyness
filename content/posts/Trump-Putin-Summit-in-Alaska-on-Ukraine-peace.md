U.S. President Donald Trump announces a summit meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin to be held on Friday, August 15, 2025, at a venue to be located in Alaska, USA. The aim of the meeting is to find a way to bring an end to the Russian invasion of Ukraine on a date no later than Sunday, August 10, 2025. The meeting between Trump and Putin took place sans Ukraine President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, who was not invited to the meeting, a development that drew criticism from Ukraine and some of its European allies, who have expressed concerns over the chances of concessions by Russia on Ukraine territory. The meeting between Trump and Putin comes at a time when Russia is launching some of the biggest attacks on Ukraine territory using ground attacks, such as the one launched on August 11, 2025, to capture Dobropillia, a town located in Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine. The meeting is also at a time when Ukraine is launching drone attacks on Russia territory. The meeting between Trump and Putin is a significant development in the Ukraine-Russia conflict, given Trump's resolve to find a way to bring an end to the conflict, which has been ongoing for a while.
