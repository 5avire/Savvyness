October 3, 2025, Hamas agreed to parts of U.S. President Donald Trump’s 20-point Gaza ceasefire plan, including hostage releases and administrative handover. 
By Tuesday, October 7, the Israeli cabinet approved the deal, and the ceasefire took effect with IDF withdrawal to agreed lines. 
Hamas began returning bodies of deceased hostages, while Israel released over 1,900 Palestinian detainees. 
The agreement marked the first major de-escalation in the conflict in nearly two years, though both sides accused each other of early violations.
