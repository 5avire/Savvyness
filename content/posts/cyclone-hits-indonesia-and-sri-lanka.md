Severe weather in Indian Ocean region has been causing major humanitarian disasters.
Cyclone Senyar is leading to catastrophic flooding in Indonesia.
Sri-Lanka is also suffering a similar fate due to Cyclone Ditwah.
The total death toll over both of them amount to over 900.

These disasters are overwhelming local emergency services as well so even getting help can become quite hard. Especially in local and remote areas, extreme weather and extreme condition can make it almost for emergency services to reach to.
This is greatly increasing the risk of large scale loss among these countries.
