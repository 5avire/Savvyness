Bulgaria officially has adopted the euro as it's currency on New year. 
It's now the 21st member of the eurozone.
The euro replaced the Bulgarian lev.

This move was hailed by EU leaders as a milestone for integration.
This adoption aims to stabilize the economy of EU's poorest member state and reduce Russia's influence.
People are anxious over if the Euro might become unstable due to this.
Despite such concerns, the European Central Bank and Bularian National Bank successfully  initiated a circulation period for both the currency till end of the month.
