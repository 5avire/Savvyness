The judicial process for the 2024 martial law crisis continues to unfold as a court in Seoul has handed down a 20-month jail term to the previous First Lady, Kim Keon Hee, on charges of corruption. This follows the harsher sentence handed down to the previous Prime Minister, Han Duck-soo, who was sentenced to 23 years in prison for having given procedural legitimacy to the martial law decree. The conservative establishment in Korea is in complete disarray as the democratic process attempts to normalize the political process in the country.
