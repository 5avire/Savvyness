S.K.'s ( South Korea ) trade ministry said that semi-conductor reached all time high sales.
The total sales was approximately around $15 Billion.  
This marked a nearly one-third increase year by year which contributed to monthly exports reaching up to $50 Billion dollars.
This significantly highlights the strength of S.K. when it comes to making chip/chipsets.

The likely cause of such an immense increase in sales is likely due to AI-related business needing high end systems. The tariffs was immense on the products, however Korea's strength in technology caused it to not be affected at all. The tariffs likely made the people not trust shady sellers and to rather directly get them from a trusted source which was Samsung of Korea. This enabled them to not be affected even though tariffs were increasing.
