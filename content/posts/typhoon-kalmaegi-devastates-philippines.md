Coastal Countries are getting hit by typhoons more and more frequently.
This subtle change is due to climate change caused by human actions.
Typhoon Kalmaegi caused catastrophic damage across Visayas and Mindanao.
The total death confirmed till now is around 142.
Over 127 are missing.

Floods, landslides and other disasters are following this typhoon.
Philippine president Bongbong Marcos declared a national state of calamity to mobilize emergency resources.
This ranks among the deadliest typhoons to ever hit Philippines.
