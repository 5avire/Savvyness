The protest in Jakartha, Indonesia which was led by students and labour unions has Escalated.
The protest is going viral all over the internet for the use of flags of "One-Piece".
The flag is meant to symbolise freedom as in the freedom the pirates have in One-Piece.
After the death of 21 year old taxi driver Affan Kurniawan, the protest took off in over 30 provinces.

The mobs turned violent after the death and looting homes of parliament members and ministers became common. The One-Piece flag was one of the main symbol they used to go against the government. President Prabowo Subianto responded to this by revoking allowances and imposing foreign travel restrictions. The protest reflected deep frustration over governance and economic issue.
