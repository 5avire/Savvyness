On July-1st, 2025, President Donald Trump officially ended it's agency for International Department.
This had a huge effect on not just the U.S. but also other countries.
It single handedly ended 64 years (approx.) of centralized American aid to 100+ countries.
This had a huge effect on a lot of countries receiving aid and help from the US.

Thid had more effect in some parts of the world such as Asia and Africa.
It has caused immediate cut-off of health, education and infrastructure support on them.
This not only signifies the loss of foreign aid from U.S.A. but also how it might handle foreign related things later on.
It has moved away from a typical "soft power" to more of a transaction based power rule by the Government.
