The results for the off-year elections are out and the results are quite remarkable.

Avigali Spanberger (Democrat) was elected the governor of Virginia and became the first female governor for a state.

Zohran Mamdani was elected the Mayor of NYC, becoming the first ever Muslim and South-American mayor of a state.

On it's own, it might sound like small achievements , however, these wins can be seen as democratic moments.
Such momentum should be carried forward and achieve diversity in Leadership.
This applies especially to countries where male are still quite seen to be more competent and dominant.
While this may not be the case in USA, it's still there for the case of Politics.
