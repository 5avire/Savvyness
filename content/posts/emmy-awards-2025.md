####The Studio#### was the most-nominated first year comedy series with 23 nominations.
The 77th Emmy Awards held on Sunday, September-14, 2025 produced quite a few groundbreaking decisions and moments.
The Studio took home the Outstanding Comedy Series award.
The Pitts took home the Outstanding Drama Series while Anthology Series went to Adolescence.

For the first time ever in the history of Emmy awards, every nominee had atleast one woman on writing team for the category of Outstanding Writing for a Comedy Series.
Owen Cooper became the youngest male Emmy winner ever.
The ceremony for the Emmy-2025 was widely praised for it's diversity and recognition of talent.
