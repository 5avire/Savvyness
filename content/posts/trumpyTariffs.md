Friday, August-1<sup>st</sup>, President Trump applied his Broader Trade Policy.
This policy brought upon many countries "tariffs". 
U.S. tariffs swept imports fromover 90 countries after a 90 day preparation pause, including 35% on some Canadian goods and 50% on Brazilian goods, with a baseline of around 30% varying by country or balance.

By today, additional escalations included increase on Indian imports to around 50% and the tariffs aimed at addressing trade deficits.
This was meant for a greater good as said by Trump, however the world has replied to this with economic concerns with potential retaliation and higher consumption costs. Reports highlighted Canada's significant hit and negotiations for some countries. Trump does not seem to want to back away from this claim of his.
