Tens of thousands of people from Bulgaria have taken part in protests against the corruption and the unbalanced budget plan.
This protest happened in between December-2 and 4,2025.
Protesters were especially mad about increase in tax and the government was accused of mismanaging public funds while normal citizens struggled even for basic necessicity.

As the protesters spread to major cities, more pressure was put on Prime Minister Rosen.
The Bulgarian Parliament withdrew the draft budget in response and was able to ease immediate tensions. However, some still continue to call for resignation of the officials.
This event showed the effect of Public Pressure and how it can influence governments decision.
