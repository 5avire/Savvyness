Meanwhile, the police in Brazil initiated an anti-crime crackdown, dubbed Operation Containment, against the strongholds of Comando Vermelho in Rio de Janeiro, resulting in a record number of at least 132 fatalities, including four police officers, and 81 arrests by November 10, 2025. This is the deadliest single police operation staged in the city. While the government lauded it as a landmark crackdown against crime syndicates, human rights activists deplored the overuse of force by the police, resulting in the loss of civilian lives.
