Magnitude 6.9 Cebu struck Philippines on Thursday, September 25,2025 causing widespread structural damage, power outage, and at least 60 confirmed death.
Thousands of civilians were affected and had to possibly relocate temporarily.

The rescue operation continued for days after the incident.
It highlighted Philippines weakness to seismic movements and had national emergency declarations and international support on site.
