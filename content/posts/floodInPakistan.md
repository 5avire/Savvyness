Heavy monsoon rains have triggered immense flooding in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province in Pakistan starting around August 14.
The total death is rising day by day and is currently at around 321 deaths.
This severe flooding has caused entire villages to be submerged, thousands to be displaced and heavy damage to infrastructure.

On August 15, a government helicopter despached for flood relief was also crashed due to bad and unstable weather.
The flood has displaced more than 100k civilians. International support for the area has been urged as urgent support. The scale of destruction is quite large and is questioning climate resilience and how pakistan prepares for such scenarios.
