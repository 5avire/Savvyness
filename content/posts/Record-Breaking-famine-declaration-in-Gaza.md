The IPC ( Integrated Food Security Phase Classification ), on Friday, August 22 officially declared famine (search what it means) in Gaza Governorate with condition in the North equally or even worse. The Israeli side disputed the findings. If condition don't change, it could spread to Deir al-Balah and Khan Younis by end of September.

This marked the first famine declaration in a conflict zone in a long time.
This triggered urgent calls for unrestricted humantarian corridors.
Starvation related to deaths are rising and showing an already catasthropic humanitarian situation.
Child malnutrition rates reached record-breaking high levels and starvation death rates also skyrocketed.
