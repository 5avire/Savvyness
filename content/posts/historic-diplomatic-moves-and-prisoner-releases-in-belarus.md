52 political prisoners, including 14 foreign nationals were released from Belarus on September-11,2025. This was done with negotiations with the US.
In response to releasing them, the US replied by lifting the sanctions on Belarusian flagcarrier Belavia.

This rare occasion amid going repression came and signaled a thaw in Belarus-US relations.
By Monday, September-15,2025, Mikola Statkevich was returned to penal colony after refusing deportation to Lithuania.
