Indian Prime Minister Modi recently reached in Jordan and made the stop of his three country tour.
The other countries included in his tour are: Ethiopia and Oman.
In Jordan, PM Modi met with King Abdullah II in Amman to discuss bilateral trade and cooperation related to security.
The main focus of his visit is related to a free-central trade agreement with Oman and to address regional stability.

The tour shows India's commitment to it's Link West policy which aims to solidify it's role as a key economic partner in Middle East and Africa  while navigating complex geopolitical dynamics of the region.
