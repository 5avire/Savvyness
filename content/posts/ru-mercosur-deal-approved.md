title = 'EU Mercosur Deal Approved'
+++

After 25 years of on-again, off-again negotiations, 21 of the 27 member states of the European Union voiced approval for the EU–Mercosur Association Agreement. This landmark deal creates the largest free trade area in the world, binding the EU with Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, and Uruguay. Such an agreement will result in a tariff-free status for 90% of goods involved in the trade, including European cars and machinery and South American agricultural produce. While heralded as a victory for diversification and economic growth, this deal continues to receive environmental scrutiny over its impact on the Amazon rainforest and opposition from French farmers.
