title = 'Coup Attempt Failed in Benin'
+++

Elements of Beninese Armed Force announced a coup attempt against President Patrice Talon.
To do this, they seized the state television in the capital, Cotonou.
It was led by Lieutenant Colonel Pascal Tigri who declared the formation of a military junta.
The coup quickly failed due to strong responses and the Nigerian AirForce carried out airstrikes against the coup forces.

Regional bloc ECOWAS also announced deployment of troops from multiple West African countries.
African countries are often thought to be poor. However, they are slowly gaining their pace and catching up. This also shows how eager African countries are to help each others in time of desperate needs as well.
