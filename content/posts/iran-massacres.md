Civil unrest persists in Iran as the first massacre of the year happens.
People are calling it the "2026 Massacres".
The massacre followed after the total shutdown of internet provided there by StarLink.
IRGC managed to shutdown Starlink satellite services and as soon as the blackout hit,
the number of death kept increasing.
A fixed number is yet to be decided.

This has taken on a regional dimension with reports of armed Kurdish fighters infiltrating from Iran to engage the security forces.
The unrest spilled over into a violent vehicle ramming attack during protest in LA in the US.
