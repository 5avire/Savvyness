title = 'Air Canada Flight Attendants Strike Resolution'
+++

The Air Canada crew, represented by the Canadian Union of Public Employees, went on strike yet again. Despite a government-imposed arbitration requirement to settle the employees down, the strike grounded all flights. 
Over 700 flights per day were delayed or grounded.
This impacted 130,000 passengers daily. 
The airline had put a halt to resumption of flights again because of the strike. 
A tentative agreement was obtained on Tuesday, August 19, 2025, allowing flights to resume gradually. 
The strike between the airline crew and the employees is a reflection of the widening gap between the administration and employees regarding the contracts and claims. 
Travelers were disrupted because of the strike.
