title = 'Israel Recognizes Somaliland'

Israel is the first United Nations member state to officially recognize the Republic of Somaliland as an independent nation.
Their joint declaration, signed by PM Benjamin Netanyahu and Somaliland President Abdirahman Mohamed Abdallah includes a pledge for Somaliland to join the Abraham accords.

This small act of recognition allows Somaliland to express itself as equals to other independent nations and act in similar manners. This also potentially provides a strategic foothold near Bab el-Mandeb to counter regional threats by cooperating with Somaliland.
This move sparked immediate critique from Somali federal government which views the recognition as an illegal aggression against its territorial integrity.
